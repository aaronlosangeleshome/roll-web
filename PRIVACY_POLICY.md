# Roll Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

**Summary:** Roll does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It has no network code.

## What Roll accesses on your device
- **Camera:** to take photos. Images are processed on your device only.
- **Photos (add only):** when you tap Save, Roll adds the finished print to your Photos library. Roll cannot read your existing photos.

## What stays on your device
Your prints, captions and edits are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases
The open source build has no purchases. In an App Store build, purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The app only learns whether a purchase is active. Payment details are never visible to the app.

## Tracking and analytics
Roll contains no third party SDKs, no advertising and no analytics.

## Children
Roll collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact
Changes to this policy are published in this repository's history. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/aaronlosangeleshome/roll-ios/issues
